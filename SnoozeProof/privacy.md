# SnoozeProof Privacy Policy

Effective: July 12, 2026

SnoozeProof is designed to work without an account or server.

## Data collection

SnoozeProof does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It does not include advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, or third-party sign-in.

## Data stored on your device

Alarm settings, mission configuration, wake history, snooze statistics, streak information, theme selection, and custom alarm sounds are stored locally on your device using Apple system frameworks. SnoozeProof Pro purchase status is provided by Apple's StoreKit and may be cached locally for offline access.

## Permissions

SnoozeProof requests permission to schedule alarms. Microphone access is requested only when you choose to record a custom alarm sound. Music Library access is requested only when you choose to import a compatible downloaded, unprotected song. Imported or recorded audio is clipped and converted locally; SnoozeProof does not upload it. Version 1 does not request camera, motion, contacts, location, health, or photo-library access.

## Purchases

Payments and purchase restoration are processed by Apple. SnoozeProof does not receive or store your payment details.

## Retention and deletion

You can delete individual alarms and wake-history entries in the app. Removing the app deletes its locally stored data, subject to normal iOS backup behavior controlled by the device owner.

## Changes

Material policy changes will be reflected in this document and the effective date above.
