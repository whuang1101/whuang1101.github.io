◆ PLAYER SUPPORT

Keep the foundry burning.

No account is required and every run saves automatically. Most problems can be resolved without losing progress.

◆ QUICK FIXES

Start here.

01

Audio is missing

Open Settings and check Master, Music, and Effects. Dice Foundry uses the iOS Playback audio route so music can continue when Silent Mode is enabled.

02

A run was interrupted

Choose Continue Run from the title screen. Progress saves automatically after battles, forge choices, routes, shop actions, and die-order decisions.

03

Animation is uncomfortable

Enable Reduce Motion / Flash in Settings. Combat timing stays the same while flashes, shakes, particles, and long transitions are reduced.

04

Text or controls are hard to read

Enable Large Text and High Contrast. Long screens scroll vertically with an ordinary upward swipe and never require side-to-side movement.

05

A purchase is not recognized

Open the Act I completion screen while online and choose Restore Purchase. Make sure the iPhone is signed into the Apple Account that made or shares the purchase.

06

The app behaves unexpectedly

Fully close and reopen Dice Foundry first. If the issue returns, the run seed and recent combat-log lines let us reproduce the exact deterministic state.
