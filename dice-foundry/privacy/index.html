◆ PRIVACY POLICY

Your run stays yours.

Dice Foundry does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. There is no account, advertising, analytics, tracking, social feature, or third-party data-collection SDK.

UpdatedJUL 252026

◆ DATA STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

Local by design.

Dice Foundry stores gameplay progress, settings, run history, unlocks, and deterministic run seeds locally on your device. This data is used only to operate the game. Removing the app may remove this local data unless iOS restores it from a device backup.

◆ RETENTION, DELETION & CHOICES

No server copy exists.

The publisher retains no gameplay or personal data on a server. You can erase Dice Foundry's local data by deleting the app from your device. iOS backup settings are controlled through your Apple Account and device settings. Because Dice Foundry does not collect personal data, there is no publisher-held account to access, export, correct, revoke consent for, or delete. You may still contact the publisher with any privacy question.

◆ PURCHASES

Handled by Apple.

The optional full-game unlock is processed by Apple through the App Store. Dice Foundry asks Apple's StoreKit service whether the non-consumable purchase is owned, but the game never receives or stores payment-card details. Apple's handling of App Store purchase information is governed by Apple's privacy policy and account settings.

◆ SERVICE PROVIDERS & SHARING

No data brokers or advertising partners.

Dice Foundry contains no advertising, analytics, social, account, or cross-app tracking SDK. The publisher does not sell or share personal data. Apple provides App Store distribution, purchase processing, Family Sharing, refunds, and optional device backup under Apple's own terms and privacy policy; the game receives only the StoreKit ownership state needed to unlock purchased content.

◆ NETWORK ACCESS

Offline gameplay.

The game requires no network connection for gameplay. A network connection may be needed when Apple processes or restores the optional App Store purchase.

◆ CHILDREN

No personal information collected.

Dice Foundry does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app is not submitted to the Made for Kids category.

◆ CHANGES

Updated before practices change.

If a future version changes these practices, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before that version is released.
