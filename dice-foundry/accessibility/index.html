◆ ACCESSIBILITY

The rules should stay readable.

Dice Foundry is designed for one-handed portrait play with visible information, adjustable presentation, and no audio-only game state.

◆ AVAILABLE IN SETTINGS

Ways to make the foundry yours.

01

Vision

Large Text, High Contrast, visible enemy intent, labeled status values, color-plus-symbol encoding, and VoiceOver names and descriptions.

02

Motion & flash

Reduce Motion / Flash removes extended travel, shake, pulsing, and strong flashes while keeping combat results understandable.

03

Hearing

Music, effects, and haptics have independent controls. Essential state is never communicated by sound alone.

04

Motor & touch

Portrait controls use mobile-sized targets, primary combat decisions stay on one page, and long supporting screens scroll vertically only.

05

Cognitive clarity

Enemy intent, seeded runs, ordered dice, one Recast, readable logs, and deterministic outcomes reduce hidden information.

06

Interruption safety

The game saves after decision points and supports pause, background, resume, and Continue Run without requiring an account.
