# RiseKeeper Sound Licenses

All six built-in RiseKeeper alarm sounds are original procedural audio generated for RiseKeeper. They contain no third-party samples and require no external attribution.

| Sound | Source |
|---|---|
| Gentle Bells | Original procedural synthesis |
| Classic Beep | Original procedural synthesis |
| Radar Rise | Original procedural synthesis |
| Energetic Synth | Original procedural synthesis |
| Birds & Chimes | Original procedural synthesis |
| Heavy Alarm | Original procedural synthesis |

The generated assets are 18-second, mono, 16-bit Linear PCM CAF files at 22.05 kHz.

Questions can be sent to [snoozeproof.help@outlook.com](mailto:snoozeproof.help@outlook.com).
