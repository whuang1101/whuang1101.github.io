Support

The important questions, answered.

Does RiseKeeper need to stay open for an alarm to ring?

No. RiseKeeper schedules through Apple AlarmKit, so the app does not need to remain open. Your iPhone still needs power, alarm permission, and compatible iOS settings.

Will my custom sound still ring after I close the app?

Yes. Compatible custom audio is converted and stored locally where the scheduled system alarm can use it. Recording inside RiseKeeper and Music Library selection require Pro; importing compatible Files or Voice Memos audio is free.

Can the iOS Stop button bypass a mission?

iOS always provides a system-controlled Stop affordance. RiseKeeper does not claim otherwise. Strict Mode can detect a bypass and re-alert, while protected snooze and Wake-Up Check add more follow-through.

Can I try premium missions before purchasing?

Yes. Typing, Memory, and Odd One Out can all be played in preview mode before you unlock Pro.

Is RiseKeeper Pro a subscription?

No. RiseKeeper Pro is a one-time, non-consumable purchase for lifetime access. Restore Purchases is available in Settings → RiseKeeper Pro.

My alarms do not appear. What should I check?

Open iOS Settings and make sure alarm access is enabled for RiseKeeper. Then open RiseKeeper once so it can reconcile saved alarms with AlarmKit. If the issue continues, contact support.

Need a human?

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description. Never send passwords or verification codes.
